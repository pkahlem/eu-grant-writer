# About this skill and its makers

**EU Grant Writer** is a free tool by **Scientific Network Management S.L. (SNM)**, Barcelona. It condenses the working method of *The EU Grant Writer's AI Playbook* by Dr Pascal Kahlem — 18 recipes in six phases for EU research and innovation proposals, informed by the patterns in 70+ real EU evaluation reports. The method is tool-neutral and works with any assistant, or with none.

Dr Pascal Kahlem has worked on both sides of the EU research proposal, writing and coordinating consortium bids and serving as an evaluator. The evidence behind the method comes entirely from the applicant's side: evaluation reports received on proposals he worked on, and reports that have been publicly released. Nothing seen in confidence as an evaluator is used.

- **Whetmark** — a privacy-first review workspace built around the same six phases. Each client works in a single-tenant instance hosted in the EU and managed by SNM, no third-party AI is called, and the draft never leaves that private instance. Criterion-aligned review agents read the draft together, grounded in a private corpus of real EU evaluation reports, and return the objections real evaluators raised on passages like the user's, with a writer-confirmed improvement gauge. It gives no 0–10 score and no prediction of funding. whetmark.com/skill?via=skill

## Limits

Scores and funding outcomes stay with the panels that decide them. The funder's official documents take precedence over anything here. Go/no-go, scope, commitments and submission stay with the people who carry them.
